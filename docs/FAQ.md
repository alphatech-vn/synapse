# FAQ

## What is Synapse?

Synapse is a hosted AI knowledge workspace built around notes, relationships, semantic retrieval, and AI synthesis.

## Who is it for?

Primary audiences include:

- founders and operators managing product and planning knowledge,
- developers keeping technical notes and project context,
- researchers and learners building connected topic networks,
- power users who want more than a plain note app.

## Is Synapse just a notes app?

No. It combines:

- a note workspace,
- multiple organizational views,
- semantic and hybrid search,
- AI chat and synthesis,
- sharing and inbound integrations.

## Does it support AI-assisted retrieval?

Yes. Synapse includes an AI assistant plus retrieval-oriented flows such as semantic search, summaries, concept extraction, and topic grouping.

## Does it include graph and relationship features?

Yes. The product includes wikilinks, backlinks, typed relations, and graph-oriented exploration.

## Can notes be shared?

Yes. The product includes sharing features, including controlled note sharing and public-link style flows where configured.

## Does this repository contain the source code?

No. This is a public product-information and support repository only.

## Is there a public self-hosted distribution?

No public self-hosted distribution flow is documented here.
