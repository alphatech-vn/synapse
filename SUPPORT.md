# Support

Synapse is a hosted product and is supported through direct contact.

## Fastest contact path

- Email: `taminhquan182@gmail.com`

## What to include when asking for help

Please send:

- the issue in one or two clear sentences,
- the page or flow where it happened,
- whether it affects login, notes, views, search, AI chat, sharing, or integrations,
- your account email if relevant,
- screenshots if the issue is visual,
- the browser or device if the issue may be environment-specific.

## What this repo is for

This repository is for:

- product information,
- public FAQ,
- roadmap visibility,
- support routing.

This repository is not the source repository for the live product.

## Recommended support flow

### Access or account issues

Use direct email for:

- login issues,
- account-access questions,
- permission problems,
- onboarding questions.

### Workspace issues

Use direct email for:

- note creation or editing issues,
- search or graph issues,
- Rosie AI or summary issues,
- share-link or integration issues,
- performance or data-behavior questions.

## Do not send publicly

Do not post or share in public:

- account credentials,
- private note content,
- API keys or tokens,
- screenshots containing sensitive business or personal information.

For anything sensitive, use email: `taminhquan182@gmail.com`.

## Response expectations

- Support is human-handled.
- Security- or privacy-sensitive reports should be sent privately.
